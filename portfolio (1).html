What We Do

Ryusei Tridents operates on two fronts — competing at the highest level we can reach, and running the tournaments other people compete in.

Competitive

Professional Valorant Roster

Our Valorant team represents Bangladesh on the regional stage, currently ranked #93 in South Asia on VLR. The team is built and coached in-house, with our caster-management crew supporting broadcast and player development alike.

Operations

Tournament & Event Management

Schools and universities across Bangladesh bring in our management team to plan and run their Valorant tournaments end-to-end — brackets, casting, server operations, and on-the-day event running. Thirteen and counting.
